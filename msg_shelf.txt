screen, shelf, principals: the last three audit gaps

the audit ended with three things prose promised and code did not do, and two modules nobody
imported. all three are closed, and the two modules turned out to be halves of one seam.

1. /screen serves a still. it used to answer 503 "no stream backend" while the spec named
   novnc/webrtc, and the celesto adapter advertised `vnc` in its bridge tuple while nothing
   served it. now GET /api/machines/:id/screen answers real png bytes with x-stream: still and
   x-frame-kind: desktop|phone. a live stream stays the runner's job and would arrive as a
   different content type rather than a relabelled poll. vnc is out of the adapter's bridge
   tuple and out of the order-form example; the schema still accepts it, and validation names
   any bridge we do not serve instead of letting an order ask for nothing.

2. warmpool.py + agentid.py have callers, which is what they were missing. acquire() has always
   demanded a stable id rather than a label, and AgentRegistry is where stable ids live.
   - POST /api/agents registers a principal and shows the seed once; prove asks the seed to
     prove the id, and renaming moves nothing
   - GET /api/pool, POST /api/pool/phones, POST /api/pool/lease, POST /api/pool/phones/:id
     release: the shelf with one identity minted per phone, one agent per lease (a second is
     "a credential mix, not sharing"), one purpose (twofa, phone-apps), and release pausing it
   - cold once, warm after: the first lease provisions the manifest, pauses, resumes, and
     writes the machine id onto the shelf record so a restart does not make the second lease a
     boot. a control plane with no provision backend refuses the lease instead of recording one
     nobody can honour, and GET /api/pool still answers because bookkeeping needs no host
   - a grant's stable_id is now checked against agentid's shape, and a grant with no stable_id
     gets a warning saying a label grants nothing

the honesty line for both: the dance is proven against a fake guest, the phone is not. no lease
has resumed a redroid shelf phone, and spec/warm-shelf.md says so with verified: false.

spec/warm-shelf.md is new and linked from the sidecar section of device-surface.md; the route
table carries the screen correction and the seven shelf routes. 330/330 on a clean clone.
